# Email Campaign Platform

An enterprise-level email campaign management platform inspired by Mailchimp and Zoho Campaigns.

---

## Dashboard Preview

![Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Features

- Authentication System
- Contact Management
- Email Template Builder
- Campaign Scheduling
- Email Analytics Dashboard
- Open & Click Tracking
- Unsubscribe Management
- Bounce & Complaint Handling
